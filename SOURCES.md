# Source and authorship

Academic work by Federico Pucci at Universidad Nacional de La Matanza. Authorship of the selected Java exercises and analysis models was confirmed by the author during publication review. Course case studies and exercise prompts remain teaching material.

The personal submission and named solution files identify Federico Pucci. The `_MIO` functions contain the student implementations; matching `funciones.h` files are course interfaces, retained with their comments. Shared helper routines may follow course examples. Instructor reference binaries and complete test drivers are not included. Personal identity numbers were removed from public copies.
